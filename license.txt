LoLStat - Terms of Use
======================

Please read before installing.

1. What LoLStat does
LoLStat runs on your PC next to League of Legends. It can accept match
ready-checks for you, lets you control that from your phone over your own
Wi-Fi, and suggests ARAM Mayhem item builds and augment rankings. It reads
information from the League client and game on your PC through the local
interfaces they provide. It does not change game files or memory.
During ARAM Mayhem, if turned on in Settings, it reads augment card names
from the game window on your screen with Windows' built-in text
recognition. Screenshots are never saved or sent anywhere.
Once a game has started, it can show other players' ranks and recent
match results from their League profiles (the same public info the client
shows). It doesn't reveal names hidden in champion select.
The Friends page shows your League friends' ranks and recent results, looked
up through your own League client. Champion themes download splash art
from Riot's Data Dragon and keep it on your PC.
LoLStat keeps an archive of your own recent games, records, goals and
journal notes on your PC to build your stats. It can show Windows
notifications, and if you turn on Tilt guard's queue pause, it cancels
matchmaking during a break you chose. Uninstalling removes LoLStat's item
sets and rune page from League if League is open.

2. Your account
LoLStat is an independent app. Riot Games' rules for third-party apps say

You use LoLStat at your own risk and are responsible for your account.

3. Data
LoLStat downloads public game data from Riot's Data Dragon and Mayhem data
from the DeepARAM project (github.com/nicothomas02/DeepARAM, MIT License,
Copyright (c) 2026 Nico Thomas). Augment rankings are shown as letter grades
only. LoLStat does not collect or send your personal data anywhere. Your
phone link key is stored only on your PC.
