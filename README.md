# LoLStat

**The all-in-one League of Legends companion for Windows.**

LoLStat sits next to League and takes care of the busywork: it accepts your matches, sets up your runes, spells and item sets in champ select, grades ARAM Mayhem augments right on your screen, scouts every player in your game, and turns your games into stats, records and challenges.

## Download

Get **LoLStat-Setup.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/Dougieee3D/LolStat/releases/latest) and run it. Each release lists what's new.

Requires Windows 10 or 11. For the in-game overlay, set League's Window Mode to **Borderless**.

## Features

- **Auto-accept** the instant a match pops, with a phone remote over your Wi-Fi.
- **Champ select:** three builds for your champion, imported as item sets (Starting Items, Core Items, Boots, Fourth and Fifth Item Options, Situational Items, Consumables & Trinkets), plus rune pages and summoner spells.
- **Build guide:** what to buy first, your first back, core items, boots, end game and situational swaps, with **Buy now** when the enemy team calls for it.
- **ARAM Mayhem:** a grade above every augment card the moment it appears, rerolls included, plus augment combos.
- **In-game panel:** your builds for the game, resizable, with your own theme or your champion skin's colors.
- **Clips:** your kills, multikills, aces, objective steals and wins recorded automatically with game sound, plus Ctrl+Shift+C to save the last 30 seconds. Watch them right in the app.
- **Party Showdown:** a best of 3 or best of 5 with your party, scored on damage to champions after every game.
- **My stats:** post-game coach, records, badges, LP graph, duos and rivals, best times to play, highlights and a season card.
- **Fun:** daily and weekly missions, Chaos mode, your augment collection and a build lab.
- Themes for every champion and skin, a friends leaderboard, mini mode, notifications, backups and more.

## Safe to install

Every release is scanned with Microsoft Defender and published with a SHA-256 checksum (`SHA256SUMS.txt`). To check your download, run `Get-FileHash LoLStat-Setup.exe` in PowerShell and compare.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
